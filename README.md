I work at the intersection of **simulation, autonomy, and interactive systems**.
My projects turn system models into testbeds: closed-loop driving stacks,
multi-robot planners, event-driven digital twins, and interfaces that make
complex behavior legible.

<a id="research"></a>
## 01 · Research focus

> **Guiding question**  
> How can simulation make complex systems more observable, testable, and understandable?

| Autonomy under uncertainty | Coordination at scale | Simulation as an interface |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Prediction, risk-aware decisions, trajectory planning, and control in closed-loop settings. | Task allocation, conflict-free routing, and repeatable evaluation for multi-agent systems. | Event-driven models made visible, inspectable, and interactive in real time. |
